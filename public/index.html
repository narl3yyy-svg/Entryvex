Click any coin or stock and Entryvex tells you, in plain English, whether to BUY, SELL or WAIT, based on what has actually made money on that market after fees, not guesses.

      Setup scanner

      What does the scanner do?
      1. It watches for 4 common trading setups (patterns traders buy on) on the coin and candle size you're viewing.
      2. For each one, it looks back at every time that setup happened in the last 3,000 candles and checks what would have happened if you bought: did it hit the profit target or the stop-loss first, after fees?
      3. That gives a measured confidence: e.g. "ended in profit 21 of 38 times". Not a guess.
      4. When a setup is active right now, you get a verdict: GO proven edge · CAUTION slight edge · UNPROVEN too few examples · NO CLEAR EDGE break-even, any profit looks like luck · NO TRADE it loses money. WAIT means no setup is active: there's nothing to do.
      5. Most of the time it says WAIT or NO TRADE. That's normal: most patterns don't beat fees. Use Find an edge to see which candle size works best for this coin, and turn on setup alerts (🔔) so you're told when a GO appears.
